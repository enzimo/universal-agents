# Operational Templates

## Contents

- Goal contract
- Governing procedure register
- Plan item
- OODA checkpoint
- Objective health review
- Delegation contract
- Recovery record
- Decision record
- Final delivery

Use only the templates that preserve state or improve a decision. Represent
them as typed runtime models when available.
